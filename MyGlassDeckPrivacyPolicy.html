Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 27, 2026

MyGlass Deck is provided by Nizamet Özkan ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how MyGlass Deck (the "App") handles information when you use the App with compatible Meta wearable devices.

1. No Personal Data Collected by Us

We do not collect, store, sell, rent, or share personal data from the App. The App does not require you to create an account with us, does not operate our own backend server, and does not use our own analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting service.

2. Data Processed on Your Device

The App may process information locally on your iPhone or compatible Meta wearable device only as needed to provide App features. Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, this may include:

  • Bluetooth, external accessory, or local network information needed to discover and connect to compatible Meta wearable devices.
  • Registration and permission status needed to connect the App through the Meta AI companion app.
  • Display content that you choose to send from the App to compatible glasses.
  • Camera stream frames or photo data from compatible glasses, if you explicitly grant camera access through Meta's permission flow and use a feature that needs it.

We do not upload this information to our servers. We do not store camera streams, photos, videos, display content, or device connection data on our servers. If the App allows you to save or export content, that action is controlled by you and may be handled by your device, iCloud, Apple services, Meta services, or another destination you choose.

3. Meta Wearables Device Access Toolkit

The App includes Meta's Wearables Device Access Toolkit for iOS so it can connect to compatible Meta glasses. Registration, device permissions, and certain device interactions may occur through the Meta AI companion app and Meta services.

Meta states that when Meta integrations are enabled, including through this SDK, Meta may collect information about how users' Meta devices communicate with an app. Meta's collection and use of that information is governed by Meta's own policies, including the Meta Privacy Policy. We do not control Meta's data practices.

You can review Meta's policies here:

  • Meta Privacy Policy
  • Meta Wearables Developer Terms
  • Meta Wearables Acceptable Use Policy

4. Permissions

MyGlass Deck may request device permissions only when they are needed for App functionality. Permission grants for Meta wearable device capabilities, such as camera access, occur through the Meta AI companion app. You can refuse permissions, revoke them where supported, unregister the App from Meta AI, disconnect your glasses, or delete the App.

5. Third-Party Services

The App relies on Apple iOS and Meta services to provide device connectivity and wearable functionality. Apple and Meta may process information under their own privacy policies. We do not receive personal data from those services except for information you voluntarily send to us, such as a support email.

6. Support Emails

If you contact us by email, we will receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, message, screenshots, or diagnostics you decide to send. We use that information only to respond to your request and provide support. We do not sell or share support emails for advertising.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us by email or another direct communication, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data Retention

Because we do not collect App data, we do not retain App usage data. Support emails may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, maintain records of support, and comply with applicable law.

9. Security

We design the App to avoid collecting data from you. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so you should use your device's security features and keep your iPhone, Meta account, and glasses secure.

10. International Users

We do not operate a server that receives App usage data. If you use Apple, Meta, or other third-party services, those services may process information in countries outside your country of residence under their own policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the App after an update means you accept the updated policy.
